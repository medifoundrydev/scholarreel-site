ScholarReel privacy policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

ScholarReel turns published research into short-form video scripts, with each claim traced to a citation. This policy explains what the app collects, what leaves your device, and how to get it deleted. It covers the Android app com.scholarreel.app.

In short. We store your sign-in identity and a daily usage counter, and nothing else. Generated scripts stay on your device. The topics you type are sent to Google's Gemini API to produce a script — see AI processing, which is the most important section here.

Who is responsible

ScholarReel is operated by an independent developer based in India. For any privacy question, complaint or data request, contact medifoundry.dev@gmail.com. We acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 15 calendar days, in line with the grievance-redressal expectations of the Indian IT Rules, 2021.

We do not use advertising, analytics SDKs, trackers, or cross-app identifiers. We do not sell personal data. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions — the app asks only for internet access.

AI processing, and what Google may do with it

When you generate a script, the topic and key points you typed are sent to Google's Gemini API, together with metadata about research papers retrieved from OpenAlex. Google generates the script and returns it.

ScholarReel currently uses the free tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use the prompts sent and the responses returned to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them. This is a meaningful difference from the paid tier and we would rather state it plainly than bury it:

  • Do not enter confidential, sensitive or personal information into the topic or key points fields.
  • We do not send your name, email address or account identifier to Google alongside your prompt.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. If we move to the paid tier, prompts will no longer be used for training, and this policy will be updated to say so.

Who else your data reaches

  • Google — sign-in, and the Gemini API as described above.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication.
  • Render — hosts our backend server.
  • OpenAlex — receives your search terms to find relevant published research. It does not receive anything identifying you.

Generated content

Scripts are produced by an AI model and may be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated. Every generated script is labelled as AI-generated in the app. Claims drawn from a study are marked with a citation you can open; lines with no citation are flagged as such. Always verify claims against the cited sources before publishing or relying on them. You can report a script from the script screen.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — and equivalent rights elsewhere — you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have it corrected
  • Have it erased, and withdraw your consent to processing
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved

You can delete your account and data at any time — in the app under Settings → Delete account, or, if you have uninstalled it, via our data deletion page.

Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account, after which it is erased permanently. Scripts on your device are kept until you delete them, delete your account, or uninstall the app.

Security

All traffic between the app, our backend and our providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Data at rest is encrypted by our hosting providers. API keys are held as server-side environment variables and are never shipped in the app.

Children

ScholarReel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially — particularly anything about the AI processing above — we will update this page and change the date at the top.
