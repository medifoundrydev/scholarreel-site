# ScholarReel

Turn published research into short-form video scripts, with every claim traced
to its source.

Enter a topic. ScholarReel searches open-access literature through
[OpenAlex](https://openalex.org), then drafts a script built from what those
studies actually found. Claims drawn from a study carry a citation you can tap
to open the original paper; lines that are framing rather than evidence are
marked as such, so you always know which parts are backed by research.

Free Android app. Currently in closed testing.
