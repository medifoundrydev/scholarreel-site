Delete your ScholarReel account and data

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your ScholarReel account (package com.scholarreel.app) and the data associated with it. You do not need the app installed to make a request.

If you still have the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately:

  • Open ScholarReel
  • Go to Settings
  • Tap Delete account and confirm

Your account is erased straight away, and the scripts stored on your device are removed at the same time.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Submit a request using the form below. Use the same email address you signed in with, otherwise we cannot match the request to an account.

Tap the button below. It opens an email addressed to us with the right subject line — send it from the address you signed in with, and we will match it to your account.

Request account deletion

If the button does not work on your device, email medifoundry.dev@gmail.com with the subject Delete my ScholarReel account.

Timeline. Requests submitted here are completed within 30 days, and usually much sooner. Deleting from inside the app is immediate — use that route if you still have it installed.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (email address, name and profile photo from Google Sign-In) Permanently deleted
Your daily generation allowance record Permanently deleted
Scripts you generated Stored only on your device, never on our servers. Removed when you delete your account in the app, or when you uninstall it.
Reports you submitted about generated content The report is kept as a safety record, but is unlinked from you so it can no longer be traced back to your account.

What we keep, and why

Nothing that identifies you is retained after deletion. Server logs may contain technical records of requests for a short period for security and abuse detection; these are not linked to your account once it is deleted, and they age out automatically.

Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its data afterwards. Signing in again with the same Google account creates a new, empty account.
