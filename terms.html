What ScholarReel does

You supply a topic. ScholarReel searches published research through OpenAlex, and uses Google's Gemini API to draft a short-form video script grounded in the studies it found, with citations you can open.

AI-generated content

Scripts are generated by an AI model. They may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or may misstate what a study actually found. Verify every claim against the cited source before publishing or relying on it. You are responsible for what you do with the output.

Lines drawn from a study carry a citation marker; lines that are general framing rather than a sourced claim are marked as such. That marking is a guide, not a guarantee — the underlying model can still make mistakes.
