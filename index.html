ScholarReel

Turn published research into short-form video scripts, with every claim traced to its source.

ScholarReel searches open-access research through OpenAlex and drafts a short-form script grounded in what those studies actually found. Every claim drawn from a study carries a citation you can open; claims that are framing rather than evidence are marked as such.

ScholarReel is a free Android app, currently in closed testing.
